# Glossary

Every domain term and its meaning. Code uses these names (ubiquitous language); a new term is added here in the pull request that introduces it.

| Term | Meaning | In code |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor unit** | The smallest unit of a currency: one centavo is 1/100 of a real. All amounts are stored and computed as whole minor units | `Money.minorUnits()` |
| **Money** | An amount in minor units together with its currency. It can be negative (a balance or a difference); a rule that needs a positive amount checks it. Amounts in different currencies never combine | [`Money`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/Money.java) |
| **Rate** | A multiplier applied to money, such as a fee percentage, held as an exact decimal (`BigDecimal`). Applying a rate is the only place money is rounded, with half-even (banker's) rounding | `Money.times(rate)` |
| **Allocation** | Splitting money into parts by ratios, such as a 70/27/3 split, so that the parts always add up to the original amount. Centavos that do not divide evenly go one each to the parts with the largest remainder | `Money.allocate(ratios)` |
| **Identifier** | A resource's primary key: a version 7 UUID, which starts with its creation time in milliseconds, so keys sort by creation and stay close together in database indexes | [`UuidV7Generator`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/UuidV7Generator.java) |
| **Public ID** | The identifier as the API shows it: a prefix naming the resource type and the UUID in 26 lowercase Crockford base32 characters ([TypeID](https://github.com/jetify-com/typeid) format), such as `ch_01h455vb4pex5vsknk084sn02q`. It is derived from the UUID, never stored separately, and parsed strictly, so each resource has one spelling | [`PublicId`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/PublicId.java) |
| **Business date** | The calendar date of an instant in `America/Sao_Paulo`, where the money settles. Instants are stored in UTC; a payment at 22:30 in São Paulo belongs to that day even though it is already the next day in UTC | [`BusinessCalendar.businessDateOf`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/BusinessCalendar.java) |
| **Bank holiday** | A national holiday on which banks do not settle, as in the ANBIMA calendar: fixed dates plus Carnival Monday and Tuesday, Good Friday and Corpus Christi, which move with Easter. State and municipal holidays are not included | [`BankHolidays`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/BankHolidays.java) |
| **Business day** | A weekday that is not a bank holiday. Pix runs every day; business days govern due dates and payout schedules, and a due date that falls on a non-business day moves to the next business day | `BusinessCalendar.isBusinessDay(date)` |
| **CPF** | The taxpayer number of a person in Brazil: 9 digits and 2 check digits. It is personal data: code reads it only where needed and prints it masked, such as `***.982.247-**` | [`Cpf`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/Cpf.java) |
| **CNPJ** | The taxpayer number of a company in Brazil: 12 characters (company and branch) and 2 check digits. From July 2026 the 12 characters may include uppercase letters (alphanumeric CNPJ); numbers already issued stay all digits. Printed masked, like a CPF | [`Cnpj`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/Cnpj.java) |
| **Pix key** | An alias registered in the Central Bank's directory (DICT) that reaches an account: a CPF, a CNPJ, an email, a mobile phone number or a random key the DICT generates. Held in the one canonical form the DICT stores, so equal keys reach the same account | [`PixKey`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/shared/PixKey.java) |
| **Account** | A named bucket of money in one currency, such as `tenant:{id}:available`. Its type (asset, liability, revenue, expense) fixes its normal balance. Accounts are created once and never changed | [`Account`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/ledger/domain/Account.java) |
| **Normal balance** | The direction that increases an account's balance: debit for assets and expenses, credit for liabilities and revenue | `AccountType.normalBalance()` |
| **Posting** | One leg of an entry: an account, a direction (debit or credit) and a positive amount in the account's currency | [`Posting`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/ledger/domain/Posting.java) |
| **Entry** | One money movement: 2 to 32 postings whose debits equal their credits in each currency. Entries are never changed or deleted | [`Entry`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/ledger/domain/Entry.java) |
| **Reversal** | An entry that undoes another: the same postings in the opposite direction, linked to the original. The only way to correct a mistake in the ledger | `Entry.reversal(id)` |
| **Charge** | A tenant's request to be paid an amount by Pix before a time. Created active; paid by one payment of its amount that the rails accepted before its expiry; partially refunded or refunded as that payment is returned | [`Charge`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/domain/Charge.java) |
| **Transaction ID** | The 25 random letters or digits that identify a charge on the rails (`txid`). The BR Code carries it and the credit notification returns it, so it is how a payment finds its charge; unique across tenants, since a notification names none | [`TransactionId`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/domain/TransactionId.java) |
| **BR Code** | The text of a charge's QR code, in the EMV format of the Central Bank's BR Code manual: two-digit tags, lengths and values, ending with a CRC16. It carries Finaxis's Pix key, the amount, the transaction ID and the merchant's name and city as a payer's app shows them | [`BrCode`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/domain/BrCode.java) |
| **Expiry** | The moment after which a payment no longer settles a charge. Judged by when the rails accepted the payment, not by when the core heard of it, so an expired charge can still be paid by a payment made in time | `Charge.expire(now)` |
| **Payment** | One Pix credit a charge received, identified by its `EndToEndId`. Either the charge's one settling payment, or a flagged one; both are credited to the tenant | [`Payment`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/domain/Payment.java) |
| **Flagged payment** | A payment credited to the tenant without settling its charge, for a reason: the charge was already paid, the payment came after the expiry, or it was of another amount. The tenant decides whether to refund it | [`ReviewReason`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/ReviewReason.java) |
| **Credit inbox** | Where the core records each Pix credit the rails notified, once by its `EndToEndId`, before answering them. A processor then hands each credit to billing at least once, retrying until it is recorded | [`CreditProcessor`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/rails/application/CreditProcessor.java) |
| **Settlement** | Turning one Pix credit into a payment of its charge and a ledger entry that credits the tenant. The charge's new status, the payment and the entry commit together or not at all, and a credit handled again has no second effect | [`Settlement`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/application/Settlement.java) |
| **Unmatched credit** | A Pix credit whose transaction ID no charge has. Its money is in Finaxis's settlement account and in no tenant's balance, so it is logged, counted and left to an operator | `CreditResult.UNKNOWN_TRANSACTION` |
| **Refund** | Money given back from one payment, in full or in part, within 90 days of the payment | [`Refund`](../../services/core/src/main/java/dev/finaxis/core/billing/domain/Refund.java) |
